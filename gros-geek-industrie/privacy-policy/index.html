Gros Geek Industrie

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Gros Geek Industrie Publisher, a private desktop tool, accesses and handles information from authorized social accounts, including Pinterest and TikTok.

Effective: August 11, 2026 Private desktop application No advertising trackers

Plain-language summary

Gros Geek Industrie Publisher is an internal tool used only by the owner of Gros Geek Industrie. It is not offered to customers or other third parties. Social-platform authorization data and publishing records are stored locally on the owner's computer. We do not sell personal data.

1. Who operates the application

Gros Geek Industrie operates Gros Geek Industrie Publisher to prepare, publish and review organic social content for its own products. The application runs locally on the operator's desktop computer.

2. Information accessed through social platforms

After the operator explicitly authorizes access through a platform's OAuth flow, the application may access:

  • basic information identifying the authorized Pinterest or TikTok account;
  • the permissions and publishing capabilities granted by the operator;
  • Pinterest boards, Pins and related metadata when Pinterest is connected;
  • TikTok profile information, uploaded media and publication status when TikTok is connected;
  • organic performance information made available by the connected platform;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

The application does not request social-platform passwords and does not collect customer payment information.

3. How the information is used

The information is used solely to:

  • authenticate the owner of the Pinterest account;
  • list available destinations when the connected platform provides them;
  • upload and publish organic product photos or videos to Pinterest and TikTok;
  • link social publications to Gros Geek Industrie product pages when the platform supports links;
  • read performance statistics for internal business analysis;
  • diagnose publication errors and prevent duplicate submissions.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and publication records are stored locally on the operator's computer and are not committed to a public source-code repository. They are retained only while the Pinterest connection or the related publication history remains useful. The operator may delete these local records at any time.

This public policy page is a static website. Gros Geek Industrie does not add cookies, advertising pixels or audience analytics to it. GitHub Pages may process basic technical connection logs under GitHub's own privacy terms as the hosting provider.

5. Disclosure and sale of data

Gros Geek Industrie does not sell, rent or trade information accessed through connected social platforms. Information is sent to the relevant platform only when required to perform an action requested by the operator, such as creating a Pin or publishing a TikTok post. Data may also be disclosed when legally required.

6. Security

Access is limited to the application operator. Credentials are excluded from source control, and the application requests only the social-platform API permissions needed for its publishing and analytics functions. No method of storage is completely risk-free, but reasonable technical measures are used to protect the locally stored authorization data.

7. Revoking access and deletion

The operator may revoke the application's access from the relevant Pinterest or TikTok account settings and may delete locally stored tokens and publication records. Revocation prevents future authenticated API requests but does not automatically delete Pins, videos or photo posts that were already published.

8. Children's data

This internal business tool is not directed to children and is not designed to collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or legal obligations change. The effective date shown above will be updated when a material revision is published.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to Gros Geek Industrie through the Gros Geek Industrie Etsy shop.
